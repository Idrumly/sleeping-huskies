# Sleeping Huskies classroom beta

A cooperative classroom sound-level game. Open the published site in a browser, select Live microphone, and enable microphone access. Demo mode works without hardware. No account required.

Audio is analysed locally, never recorded or uploaded by the game. No camera access, analytics, student accounts, or remote score storage. The hosting provider may keep normal web access logs. Scores last only for the current session.

Beta 0.3.0: a layered animated family with sleeping, stirring, wriggling, and awake poses; individual pup stretches, rolls and head tilts; independent breathing rhythms and intermittent movements; mom covers her ears at the highest warning level; optional synthesized whimper warnings with volume and preview. In microphone mode, focus time and wake-up freeze for 2.8 seconds around each warning to avoid speaker feedback. Warnings are muted by default, rearm after settling, and have a 20-second cooldown. Classroom calibration is still in development.

Refresh to load updates. Microphone settings depend on the browser and device.
